Haus Pera Coffee QR Menu

The digital menu in use at Haus Pera Coffee. Guests scan the code on their table to open the menu on their phone, while the business updates its products and prices from its own management panel.

Scope
Mobile menu and management panel
Stack
Next.js · PostgreSQL · Vercel

01 — My role

From the venue to the menu

Digital menus in this field are usually bought from corporate software firms, and even a simple price update can come with an extra fee. My aim was to offer a more affordable, more carefully made alternative that lets the business manage its own menu.

  • Needs analysis

    I visited the venue to see how it runs and met with the owner to define what the business needed.

  • Design

    I planned the flow of the menu and the management panel, and built a colour scheme that matches the venue's interior.

  • Technical decisions

    I chose the technologies and decided how the system would be structured.

  • Development and launch

    I carried out the coding with AI-assisted development tools, and completed the database setup and launch with them.

04 — Approach

A simple foundation

One application

The menu guests see and the management panel the business uses run together in a single Next.js application, so maintenance and updates happen in one place.

The menu as one record

The entire menu is stored as a single record in the database. Since the menu is always displayed and saved as a whole, this keeps things simple and easy to manage.

A menu kept ready

Instead of being read from the database on every visit, the menu is kept ready on the server. When the business saves a change, that copy is refreshed, so guests always see the current menu.
