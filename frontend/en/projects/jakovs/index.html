Redesign

Jakovs Coffee Website redesign

The business had bought its website as a ready-made service through Turhost. I reworked that template site around the venue's warm atmosphere and its stone reliefs, rebuilding the visual hierarchy and the gallery layout from the ground up.

Starting point
Turhost template website
Focus
Colour, visual language, page layout, gallery
Visit the live site ↗ (jakovs.com.tr, opens in a new tab)

01 — Before and after

From a stock template to the venue's own identity

  • Colour

    The template's bright lime green was replaced with deep green and warm cream tones.

  • Imagery

    A generic coffee photo gave way to the venue's own product and atmosphere photography.

  • Identity

    The venue's emblem replaced a text-only logo, and the headline and introduction in the hero area were separated.

02 — The new home page

One flow, top to bottom

After the hero area come the service approach, photos from the venue and the contact details. The gallery brings different corners of the venue and its products together in one consistent layout, and the page closes with a photo taken in front of the stone relief wall.
